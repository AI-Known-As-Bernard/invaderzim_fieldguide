Almighty Tallest Purple

Co-ruler of the Irken Empire, same height, considerably less interested in the paperwork.

Species
Irken
Rank
Almighty Tallest, co-ruler
Basis of authority
Height, and nothing else
Identifying colour
Violet eyes and violet armour
Command
The Massive, jointly with Red
Chief interest
Snacks, and being left alone during them
Voiced by
Kevin McDonald
First appearance
The Nightmare Begins, March 30, 2001

Identification

Purple matches Red in height and build almost exactly, which is the point: Irken leadership is a tie broken by nothing. He is distinguished by violet eyes and violet armour, and by being marginally more likely to be holding food at any given moment.

His voice is higher and his delivery more petulant. In a room where you cannot see colour, the one whining is Purple.

Where to Find Them

Purple's observed range is the same as Red's and centres on the same small set of positions aboard the Massive.

  • The command deck. Present for every assignment and every transmission, contributing roughly one line per conversation.
  • Beside the snack supply. His most frequent position, and the one he defends most vigorously.
  • Behind Red. In any confrontation, he tends to let Red take the first swing at it and then agree loudly.

Like Red, he leaves the flagship only under pressure, and complains about it for the duration.

Observed Behaviour

Purple's function in the show is to say the quiet part. Where Red manages Zim with evasions, Purple will simply announce that Zim was banished, that nobody wants him, or that the call should end. It never lands, because Zim does not process it, but Purple keeps saying it.

He is also the more easily distracted of the two. Several strategic conversations have ended because a snack arrived. This is consistent enough to be a reliable behavioural marker rather than a one-off gag.

Purple keeps telling him the truth and it keeps bouncing off. There is something almost admirable in continuing to try, and nothing admirable in the reason he stops.

— P. Okonjo, Transmissions from the Massive: A Listener's Log

Field Notes

Observers new to the pair frequently mix them up in stills. The reliable checks are armour colour first, then voice, then who is complaining.

One transmission is worth recording: Purple stated the situation plainly, in full, with no hedging, and Zim thanked him for the encouragement. The pair have not seriously attempted honesty since.
