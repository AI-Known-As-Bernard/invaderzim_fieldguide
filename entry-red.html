Almighty Tallest Red

Co-ruler of the Irken Empire on the strength of being tall. The one doing most of the talking.

Species
Irken
Rank
Almighty Tallest, co-ruler
Basis of authority
Height, and nothing else
Identifying colour
Red eyes and red armour
Command
The Massive, flagship of the Irken fleet
Chief responsibility
Assigning Invaders and overseeing Operation Impending Doom II
Voiced by
Wally Wingert
First appearance
The Nightmare Begins, March 30, 2001

Identification

Red is enormously tall and extremely thin, with a narrow head, long curling antennae, and red eyes. His armour is red where Purple's is violet, and that colour is the fastest way to tell the two apart at a distance, because their build is otherwise close to identical.

He is usually floating rather than standing, and usually holding a snack. Neither of these is a costume detail; both are load-bearing facts about how the Irken ruling class operates.

Where to Find Them

Red is almost never off the Massive, and within it he keeps to a small number of positions.

  • The command deck. In front of the main viewscreen, where invasion assignments are handed out and progress reports are received.
  • The snack supply. A recurring destination. The Empire's logistics appear to prioritise it.
  • The Great Assigning. The ceremonial event where Invaders receive their planets, and the origin of the entire Zim problem.

He has left the ship on Earth-related business exactly as often as he could not avoid it, which is very rarely.

Observed Behaviour

Red is the strategist of the pair, in the narrow sense that he is the one who thinks of something. Sending Zim to a planet nobody had heard of was his solution to a problem he could not solve any other way, and it worked exactly as well as it deserved to.

His dominant mode is exhausted sarcasm. He takes Zim's transmissions, listens to about a sentence, and spends the rest of the call looking for a way to end it. On the occasions he tries to be direct about Zim's status, Zim reinterprets it as praise and Red gives up.

The Empire's entire command structure is two people who cannot bring themselves to hang up. Everything that follows is downstream of that.

— P. Okonjo, Transmissions from the Massive: A Listener's Log

Field Notes

A useful detail for identification work: the Tallest were visually redesigned after the pilot, so early footage shows different armour. Cataloguers working from mixed sources should date their images.

Red has, on record, approved a plan to destroy Zim's ship, invented a fake secret mission, and simply stopped answering calls. None of it worked. He has never once tried telling Zim plainly that he was banished, which is the only method that has not been attempted.
