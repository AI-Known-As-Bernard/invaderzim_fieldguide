Dib Membrane

The only person on Earth who noticed that the new kid is green. Self-appointed paranormal investigator, permanently unbelieved.

Species
Human
Age
Skool-age child
Occupation
Paranormal investigator, self-appointed
Affiliation
The Swollen Eyeball Network, under the handle Agent Mothman
Family
Professor Membrane (father), Gaz (sister)
Standard equipment
Camera, audio recorder, and whatever he can build in an afternoon
Voiced by
Andy Berman
First appearance
The Nightmare Begins, March 30, 2001

Identification

Dib is a pale, glasses-wearing child in a black trenchcoat, with hair that comes to a single forward-curving point. He is easy to identify at a distance because he is usually holding a camera, and easier still to identify up close because he will immediately begin explaining what he has photographed.

He is the son of the most famous scientist alive, which shapes everything about him. He uses the language of evidence and proof, builds real equipment, and files real reports. The problem is never his method. The problem is his audience.

Where to Find Them

Dib is rarely far from Zim, and the reverse is also true. Three locations account for nearly all sightings.

  • Ms. Bitters' classroom. Seated across from Zim, alternating between taking notes and being ignored by everyone in the room.
  • His bedroom. Wall-to-wall photographs, pinned notes, and equipment, plus a computer permanently logged in to the Swollen Eyeball Network.
  • The perimeter of Zim's base. Usually at night, usually with a camera, usually about to be detected by a lawn gnome.

If he is not at any of the three, check the local news for a story about a child who interrupted a broadcast with an alien photograph.

Observed Behaviour

Dib's defining trait is that he does not stop. He has been laughed out of a classroom, dismissed by his own father, physically thrown out of a television studio, and sent back to the base the same night with new equipment. The show treats this as both his heroism and his problem.

His failures follow a pattern worth documenting: he obtains genuine proof, and then loses it to circumstance rather than to Zim's countermeasures. Cameras break, files corrupt, witnesses wander off. On the occasions when the evidence survives, the adults look directly at it and decide it is something else.

Give the boy one credulous adult and the invasion ends that afternoon. The Empire is not being defeated by Zim's incompetence alone; it is being protected by everyone who will not look up.

— Marla Vex, Notes from the Skool Perimeter

Field Notes

Field researchers should be aware that Dib will attempt to recruit you. He interprets any interest in his subject as agreement and will begin assigning tasks within a few minutes of meeting you.

He is also the most reliable source of information in this guide. His notes are dated, his photographs are catalogued, and his account of events has matched independent observation every time it has been checked. Treat his conclusions with the same caution you would treat anyone's; treat his data as sound.
