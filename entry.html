Zim

Irken invader, unauthorized. Currently operating out of a suburban house with a purple roof and a lawn full of armed gnomes.

Species
Irken
Height
Very short by Irken standards, which in Irken society is a disqualifying trait
Home planet
Irk
Assignment
Conquest of Earth — self-declared, never officially issued
Base of operations
A house on a residential street in an unnamed American town
Known associates
GIR, the house Computer, Minimoose
Primary weakness
Water, meat, and being told he is short
First recorded
March 30, 2001

Identification

Zim is roughly the height of an elementary school student, with green skin, large red compound eyes, and two thin antennae that flatten backward when he is angry, which is most of the time. In uniform he wears a magenta tunic with black gloves and boots, and carries a domed PAK on his back that houses his memory, life support, and a set of retractable mechanical legs.

His human disguise consists of a pair of contact lenses and a black wig. It fools every adult he encounters. The only reliable identification method in the field is behavioural: Zim narrates his own plans aloud, at volume, to no one.

Where to Find It

Zim divides his time between three locations, and an observer who checks all three will find him at one of them.

  • The house. Green, purple-roofed, and surrounded by lawn gnomes that are actually laser turrets. The upper floors are decoration; the real base is underground.
  • The Skool. He attends Ms. Bitters' classroom daily, seated across from Dib, in what he describes as deep-cover infiltration.
  • Anywhere Dib is. Their rivalry is the reliable one in his life, and he will abandon an active plan to interfere with one of Dib's.

Approach is not recommended during a transmission to the Almighty Tallest. He is at his loudest and least aware of his surroundings while filing a progress report.

Observed Behaviour

Zim's plans are ambitious, well-resourced, and undone almost every time by a detail he considered beneath his attention. He has moved the planet, hollowed out the Earth's core, replaced the population's organs, and shrunk himself to microscopic size, and each attempt collapsed for a small reason he refused to check.

The central fact of the character is that he does not know he was exiled. The Tallest sent him to a planet they believed did not exist, specifically so he would leave. Every triumphant report he transmits is received as an annoyance, and he has never once read the room.

He is not failing because the mission is difficult. He is failing because nobody ever gave him one, and he refuses to notice the silence on the other end of the line.

— Marla Vex, Notes from the Skool Perimeter

Field Notes

Two encounters are worth recording. In the first, Zim spent an entire afternoon constructing a device to broadcast his victory across the galaxy, then could not operate it because GIR had filled the housing with nachos. He blamed Dib.

In the second, a light rain began during an outdoor operation. Irken physiology reacts to water the way human skin reacts to acid, and the operation ended in under a minute. Anyone documenting Zim in the field should check the forecast; a drizzle is the most effective countermeasure available.
