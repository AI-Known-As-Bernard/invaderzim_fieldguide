Tak

A trained Irken denied Invader status because of Zim, arriving on Earth with a better plan and better equipment.

Species
Irken
Status
Rogue. Never granted Invader rank
Grievance
A power failure caused by Zim cost her the Invader examination
Companion unit
MIMI, a functioning and obedient SIR-type robot
Human disguise
A purple-haired skool student
Equipment
A spacecraft substantially more capable than Zim's Voot Cruiser
Voiced by
Olivia d'Abo
First appearance
TAK: The Hideous New Girl, May 15, 2002

Identification

Out of disguise, Tak is an Irken of ordinary height with violet eyes and antennae that curl at the tips. Her armour is dark and close-fitting rather than the bright uniform Zim wears, which is itself informative: she is not operating under Empire authority and is not dressed as though she is.

In disguise she passes as a human student with purple hair and pale skin. The disguise is materially better than Zim's and holds up under scrutiny, including Dib's.

Where to Find Them

Tak's Earth activity is brief but concentrated, and it centres on three places.

  • The Skool. Where she establishes her cover, positioned close enough to Zim to monitor him without being noticed.
  • Her underground base. Better equipped and better hidden than Zim's, and running an actual plan on a schedule.
  • Zim's base. Which she infiltrates directly, because unlike Dib she has no interest in gathering proof first.

Her ship remains on Earth after she does not. Dib recovers it, wipes its personality, and installs his own, which goes about as well as that description suggests.

Observed Behaviour

The behaviour that matters here is planning. Tak arrives with a goal, a cover, a working robot, a base, and a schedule, and she executes against them. She is the only antagonist in the guide whose scheme fails because of interference rather than because of a flaw she built into it herself.

Her motive is entirely personal. She is not conquering Earth for the Empire; she is doing it to be recognised, and specifically to be recognised as the thing Zim was handed by accident. That makes her the clearest mirror the show ever holds up to its lead.

She wanted the job he was given to get rid of him. Nobody in the Empire ever explained that to either of them, and the whole disaster runs on that one withheld sentence.

— Hollis Renn, A Suburban Bestiary

Field Notes

MIMI is worth documenting separately for anyone extending this guide. She is what GIR was supposed to be — silent, precise, and completely obedient — and the contrast is deliberate.

For identification purposes: Tak's Earth appearances are few, so most available images show her in the human disguise. Anyone building a photo reference should label which form is pictured, because the two are easy to file incorrectly.
