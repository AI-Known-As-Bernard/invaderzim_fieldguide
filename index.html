A Field Guide to Invader ZIM

Between 2001 and 2006, a defective Irken soldier landed in a quiet American suburb and began a conquest that nobody asked for. This guide catalogues the nine characters who make that story work, from the invader himself to the sister who could end it in an afternoon and has chosen not to.

Every character gets a full entry page with a specification sheet, identification notes, observed behaviour, and where to look for them. Nine entries, nine pages, one shared structure.

Character of the Month: GIR

GIR is a Standard-issue Information Retrieval unit, or SIR, assembled from spare parts and scrap by the Almighty Tallest as a joke at Zim's expense. Where a functioning SIR unit provides tactical analysis and mission support, GIR provides commentary about waffles.

He operates in two observable states. In duty mode his eyes and markings glow red, his posture straightens, and he executes orders with unsettling precision. The rest of the time he glows cyan, wears a green dog suit as a disguise, and pursues whatever crosses his field of vision — usually food, television, or a small pig.

Field researchers should note that GIR is the single greatest obstacle to Zim's conquest of Earth, and neither of them appears to have noticed.

The Collection

  • Zim

    Irken · Invader (unauthorized)

    An Irken soldier of famously small stature who was exiled by leaders hoping he would never come back. He interpreted the exile as a promotion and has been attempting to conquer Earth ever since. Every plan he has ever launched has collapsed over a detail he considered beneath his attention.

    Read the full Zim entry

  • GIR

    SIR unit · Defective

    Zim's robot assistant, assembled from garbage by the Almighty Tallest and presented as advanced technology. He switches between a red-eyed duty mode and a cyan-eyed default state in which he is interested only in snacks and television. His green dog suit is accepted as a complete disguise by everyone on the street.

    Read the full GIR entry

  • Dib Membrane

    Human · Paranormal investigator

    Dib has spent years trying to expose Zim with photographs, recordings, and increasingly elaborate traps. He is usually right about everything and almost never believed, largely because the adults around him refuse to look at the evidence. His persistence is the only thing keeping Zim's invasion in check.

    Read the full Dib Membrane entry

  • Gaz Membrane

    Human · Competitive gamer

    Gaz is almost never separated from her handheld console and reacts to interruption the way a wasp reacts to a shaken nest. She is fully aware that Zim is an alien and has decided the matter is not worth her attention. On the rare occasions she does get involved, the situation resolves immediately.

    Read the full Gaz Membrane entry

  • Professor Membrane

    Human · Scientist

    Membrane is credited with most of the technology that keeps the world running, and he cannot spare the ten minutes it would take to look at his son's evidence. He dismisses Dib's alien research as insanity while casually inventing things that are far stranger. His absence is the engine of most of the family's plots.

    Read the full Professor Membrane entry

  • Ms. Bitters

    Species unconfirmed · Skool educator

    Ms. Bitters moves without visible steps, dissolves into shadow, and delivers a curriculum consisting largely of doom. She has been at the Skool for an unspecified and apparently very long time. Nobody in the building treats any of this as unusual.

    Read the full Ms. Bitters entry

  • Almighty Tallest Red

    Irken · Ruling class

    Red commands the Empire's invasions from the bridge of the Massive alongside Purple, and holds the position for the only qualification Irken government recognises: height. He is the sharper and more sarcastic of the pair, and the one who usually decides how to get rid of Zim. Most of his screen time is spent managing a transmission he does not want.

    Read the full Almighty Tallest Red entry

  • Almighty Tallest Purple

    Irken · Ruling class

    Purple rules alongside Red and brings a different energy to it: less scheming, more complaining, and a much more direct relationship with the snack supply. He is the one who says out loud what the pair are actually thinking about Zim. He is also the one most likely to be holding a drink while an invasion collapses.

    Read the full Almighty Tallest Purple entry

  • Tak

    Irken · Rogue invader

    Tak was taking her Invader examination when a blackout Zim caused left her trapped underground for decades. She arrives on Earth disguised as a human student, running a plan that is genuinely well-designed and nearly succeeds. Her competence is the point of the character: she demonstrates what Zim was supposed to be.

    Read the full Tak entry

How These Characters Were Chosen

Invader ZIM has a large cast for a show that ran twenty-seven episodes, and a guide that tried to include everyone would end up as a list of names instead of a set of observations. I set one rule: a character earns an entry if removing them would change how the show works, not just which jokes land.

That rule kept the nine here and cut several favourites. Keef, the Roboparents, MIMI, Minimoose, and the Resisty are all genuinely funny, but the series would run more or less the same without them. Tak, by contrast, exists to demonstrate what a competent invader looks like, which is the whole reason Zim's failures read as failures.

Red and Purple get separate entries rather than a shared one, because the joke only works if you can see the difference between them: Red manages Zim with evasion and Purple manages him by telling the truth nobody hears. Filed together, that contrast disappears. Every entry is written from the broadcast episodes rather than the comics or the 2019 film, so the notes stay consistent with a single source.
