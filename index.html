Invader Zim Fieldguide

Between 2001 and 2006, defective Irken soldier landed in a quiet American suburb and began a conquest that nobody asked for. This guide catalogues nine of the specimens involved, from the invader himself to the robot who loves tacos more than the mission

Each entry lists spevies, allegiance, base of operations, and the behaviour you are most likely to observe in the wild. Bring snacks. Do not make eye contact with the educator.

Specimen of the Month: Gir

GIR is a standard-issue Information Retrieval Unit, or SIR, assembled from spare parts and scrap by the Almight Tallest as a joke at Zim's expense. Where a functioning SIR unit provides tactical analysis and mission support, GIR provides commentarty about waffles.

He operates in two observable states. In duty mode his eyes and markings glor red, his posture straightens, and he executes orders with unsettling precision. The rest of the time he glow cyan, wears a green dog suit as a disguise, and persues whatever crosses his gield of vision - usually food, television, or a small pig

Field reseachers should note that GIR is the single greatest obstacle to Zim's conquest of Earth, and neither of them appears to have noticed

The Collection

  • Zim

    Irken · Invader (unauthorized)

    An Irken soldier of famously small stature who was exiled to a distant planet by leaders who hoped he would never come back. He interpreted the exile as a promotion. He has been attempting to conquer Earth ever since, from a house with a purple roof and gnome-shaped defence turrets.

    Read full entry

  • GIR

    SIR Unit · Defective

    Zim's robot assistant, built from garbage and issued as a prank. He switches between a red-eyed duty mode and a cyan-eyed default state in which he is interested only in snacks and television. His green dog suit is considered a complete disguise by everyone on the street.

    Read full entry

  • Dib Membrane

    Human · Paranormal investigator

    The only person on Earth who noticed that the new kid is green and has no ears. Dib has spent years trying to expose Zim with photographs, recordings, and increasingly elaborate traps. Every attempt fails, usually because the adults around him refuse to look at the evidence.

    Read full entry

  • Gaz Membrane

    Human · Competitive gamer

    Dib's younger sister, and the most dangerous person in the guide by a considerable margin. Gaz is almost never separated from her handheld console and reacts to interruption the way a wasp reacts to a shaken nest. She is fully aware that Zim is an alien and has decided it is not worth her time.

    Read full entry

  • Professor Membrane

    Human · Scientist

    The most celebrated scientist on the planet and a distant, permanently busy father to Dib and Gaz. He appears at dinner as a hologram and dismisses his son's alien research as "insanity." His actual inventions include free energy, food that cooks itself, and at least one working portal.

    Read full entry

  • Ms. Bitters

    Species unconfirmed · Skool educator

    The teacher assigned to Zim and Dib's classroom, and the strongest argument in this guide that not every specimen can be classified. She moves without visible steps, dissolves into shadow, and delivers lessons consisting mainly of doom. Skool records list her as having taught there for a very long time.

    Read full entry

  • The Almighty Tallest

    Irken · Ruling class

    Red and Purple rule the Irken Empire on the strength of being taller than everyone else, which is the entire basis of Irken government. They command the invasion of the galaxy from a snack-filled command deck. Most of their screen time is spent avoiding Zim's transmissions.

    Read full entry

  • Tak

    Irken · Rogue invader

    A trained Irken who was denied invader status after Zim accidentally sabotaged her exam and stranded her for decades. She arrives on Earth disguised as a human student with a far better plan and far better equipment than Zim has ever assembled. Her competence is the point of the character.

    Read full entry

  • Minimoose

    Irken technology · Companion unit

    A small floating robotic moose introduced late in the series as Zim's newest assistant. Minimoose communicates entirely in squeaks that only Zim claims to understand, and never explains itself. Whatever it is actually capable of has never been fully documented on screen.

    Read full entry

How These Entries Were Chosen

Invader ZIM has a large cast for a show that ran two seasons, and a guide that tried to include everyone would end up as a list of names instead of a set of observations. I set one rule: a specimen earns an entry if removing it would change how the show works, not just which jokes land.

That rule kept the nine here and cut several favourites. Keef, the Roboparents, and the Resisty are all genuinely funny, but the series would run more or less the same without them. Tak, by contrast, exists to demonstrate what a competent invader looks like, which is the whole reason Zim's failures read as failures.

Each entry is written from observation of the broadcast episodes rather than from supplementary comics or the 2019 film, so the notes stay consistent with a single source. Where the show contradicts itself — Ms. Bitters is the obvious case — the entry says so instead of picking a side.
